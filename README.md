# Lantern Below

**A dark, top-down action roguelike set beneath the city of Ossolia.** Light is your vision, your weapon, and a resource you must manage carefully.

🎮 **[Play in your browser](https://fullystackdddd.itch.io/lantern-below)**

## About

Descend into a dangerous underground world, face creatures of shadow, collect Lightcores, and bring light back to Ossolia. Explore changing dungeons, meet the town’s residents, and uncover hints of the mystery hidden in the abyss.

## Features

- Lantern-based light and fuel management
- Combat, dashing, health, and shadow enemies
- Procedurally generated dungeons and treasure rooms
- Ossolia town hub, NPCs, dialogue, and progression
- Graphics, audio, language, and control settings
- Ukrainian, English, and Russian language options

## Built with

- Godot 4
- GDScript
- AI-assisted development (vibe coding)
